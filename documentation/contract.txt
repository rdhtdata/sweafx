## 1. BACKGROUND AND PURPOSE

1.1. SweafEx wishes to commission a digital operations software platform to support its freight brokerage and related operational workflows, including customer and transporter management, shipment operations, document handling, reporting, role-based workspaces and agreed external integrations.

1.2. The Service Provider has agreed to develop, implement, test and deploy Phase 1 of the SweafEx Digital Operations Platform in accordance with this Agreement and the approved project scope.

1.3. The Parties have agreed to a fixed development fee of INR 3,00,000 (Indian Rupees Three Lakh only), exclusive of applicable GST and other legally chargeable taxes, subject to the terms of this Agreement.

1.4. The Parties intend that SweafEx shall own the specifically commissioned Project-Specific Deliverables in accordance with the intellectual property provisions of this Agreement, while the Service Provider shall retain ownership of its Background Technology and the expressly reserved rights to reuse and commercialise generic and reusable technology.

1.5. This Agreement supersedes inconsistent commercial statements in earlier proposals only to the extent expressly provided herein. The approved project scope shall remain governed by Schedule 1, subject to this Agreement and any mutually signed change requests.

## 2. DEFINITIONS

For this Agreement:

2.1. “**Platform**” means the SweafEx Digital Operations Platform developed under this Agreement.

2.2. “**Project-Specific Deliverables**” means the bespoke source code, object code, custom workflows, custom software modules, interface designs, technical documentation and other specifically commissioned materials expressly included in Schedule 1 and created for SweafEx under this Agreement, excluding Background Technology and Third-Party Materials.

2.3. “**Background Technology**” means technology owned, developed, acquired or lawfully licensed by the Service Provider independently of this Agreement, including pre-existing software, libraries, frameworks, reusable modules, templates, generic workflow components, development tools, methodologies and general technical know-how.

2.4. “**Third-Party Materials**” means software, libraries, APIs, services, datasets or other materials owned or controlled by third parties and incorporated into or used in connection with the Platform.

2.5. “**Milestone**” means a development stage described in Schedule 2.

2.6. “**Production Go-Live**” means the date on which the agreed Phase 1 Platform is first made available for operational use in the production environment designated for SweafEx, as recorded in writing by the Parties.

2.7. “**Hypercare Period**” means the forty-five (45) calendar-day period commencing on Production Go-Live, subject to the provisions of Clause 8.

2.8. “**Business Day**” means a day other than a Sunday or public holiday on which banks are ordinarily open for business at the agreed payment location in India.

## 4. DEVELOPMENT FEE AND PAYMENT TERMS

### 4.1. Fixed development fee

The total development fee for Phase 1 shall be INR 3,00,000 (Indian Rupees Three Lakh only), exclusive of applicable GST and other legally chargeable taxes.

### 4.2. Payment schedule

SweafEx shall pay the development fee in the following instalments:

| Payment | Description | Percentage | Amount |

### 4.3. Advance payment

4.3.1. The advance of INR 60,000 shall be paid against the Service Provider's invoice or payment request and must be received in cleared funds before project commencement.

4.3.2. The Service Provider shall have no obligation to mobilise the development team, commence substantive development or reserve a commencement date until the advance has cleared.

4.3.3. The advance forms part of, and is not additional to, the total development fee.

### 4.4. Milestone payment procedure

4.4.1. Upon completion of each Milestone, the Service Provider shall demonstrate the relevant working deliverables to SweafEx and provide a written notice that the Milestone is ready for review.

4.4.2. SweafEx shall review the demonstrated deliverables within five (5) Business Days of the demonstration or receipt of the review materials, whichever is later.

4.4.3. If SweafEx identifies a material deviation from the agreed Milestone requirements, it shall provide a written notice specifying the deviation in reasonable detail within the review period. The Service Provider shall be given a reasonable opportunity to correct the identified deviation and resubmit the affected deliverables.

4.4.4. If SweafEx does not provide a written rejection identifying a material deviation within the review period, the Milestone shall be deemed accepted for purposes of the milestone payment, provided the Service Provider has supplied the agreed review materials and made the working demonstration reasonably available.

4.4.5. Upon acceptance or deemed acceptance, the Service Provider may issue the relevant milestone invoice. SweafEx shall pay that invoice within five (5) Business Days of receipt, together with applicable taxes.

4.4.6. A rejection shall not be valid merely because SweafEx requests new functionality, a design preference change, an additional report or a feature outside the agreed Milestone requirements.

4.4.7. The Service Provider shall not be obliged to commence the next Milestone until the full payment due for the preceding Milestone has been received in cleared funds.

4.4.8. The Milestone 5 payment shall be due following acceptance or deemed acceptance of its agreed review deliverables and before Production Go-Live and final production handover. The Service Provider shall not be required to place the Platform into production or complete the final handover while that payment remains outstanding, except as otherwise expressly agreed in writing.

### 4.5. Taxes and invoicing

4.5.1. The development fee excludes GST and other applicable taxes legally chargeable on the Services. Such taxes shall be charged in accordance with applicable law against a valid invoice.

4.5.2. Each Party shall be responsible for its own income taxes and other taxes imposed on it by law. Any tax deduction or withholding legally required from a payment shall be made by SweafEx in accordance with applicable law, with the relevant certificate or evidence furnished to the Service Provider.

4.5.3. The Parties shall exchange the tax registration details reasonably required for invoicing and compliance. The Service Provider shall not represent that GST is chargeable under a particular registration unless legally entitled to do so.

### 4.6. Payment default and suspension

4.6.1. If SweafEx fails to pay an amount by its due date, the Service Provider may issue written notice identifying the outstanding amount and requesting payment.

4.6.2. If the overdue amount remains unpaid for three (3) Business Days after such notice, the Service Provider may suspend development, testing, deployment, handover or other affected Services until the overdue amount is received in cleared funds.

4.6.3. The Service Provider shall not be liable for delays, missed target dates or additional remobilisation time resulting from a payment default or suspension permitted under this Agreement.

4.6.4. The implementation schedule shall be extended by the period of the suspension and the reasonable period required to remobilise resources and resume work, taking account of team availability and project dependencies.

4.6.5. Any interest on overdue amounts shall be payable only at the rate expressly agreed in writing by the Parties or otherwise recoverable under applicable law. No additional contractual interest rate is created by this Clause.

4.6.6. Suspension shall not waive SweafEx's obligation to pay amounts properly due for accepted Milestones or other accrued contractual obligations.

## 8. HYPERCARE AND POST-IMPLEMENTATION MAINTENANCE

### 8.1. Hypercare commencement and duration

8.1.1. The Service Provider shall provide forty-five (45) consecutive calendar days of hypercare commencing on the Production Go-Live date.

8.1.2. Hypercare is included in the fixed development fee and does not attract an additional hypercare fee, subject to the limitations in this Clause.

### 8.2. Hypercare scope

During the Hypercare Period, the Service Provider shall investigate and correct reproducible defects in the delivered Project-Specific Deliverables that cause the Platform materially to fail to conform to the agreed scope.

Hypercare includes reasonable technical investigation and stabilisation of the deployed Phase 1 Platform within the agreed scope.

### 8.3. Hypercare exclusions

Hypercare does not include:

(a) new features, enhancements or additional modules;

(b) changes to approved workflows or requirements;

(c) additional integrations not included in the agreed scope;

(d) third-party provider charges or infrastructure costs;

(e) issues caused by unauthorised modifications, misuse, Client-side configuration changes or unsupported environments;

(f) outages or failures caused solely by independent third-party services; or

(g) ongoing operational staffing, continuous monitoring or a guaranteed uptime commitment, unless separately agreed in writing.

Where the cause of an issue is uncertain, the Service Provider shall make reasonable efforts to investigate it. Work outside the included hypercare scope shall be quoted separately where appropriate.

### 8.4. Issue reporting and response

SweafEx shall report issues through the designated communication channel and provide sufficient information to reproduce or investigate them. The Parties shall agree a primary contact and reasonable issue-prioritisation and communication arrangements before Production Go-Live.

The Parties shall cooperate in good faith to resolve material defects promptly. No specific response-time or resolution-time service level is created by this Clause unless separately recorded in writing.

### 8.5. Maintenance after hypercare

8.5.1. Upon expiry of the Hypercare Period, ongoing paid maintenance and support shall be governed exclusively by a separate written maintenance agreement signed by both Parties.

8.5.2. The Parties acknowledge that the earlier commercial proposal referenced INR 20,000 per month for maintenance under Option A. That amount is not binding under this Agreement and shall not become payable automatically.

8.5.3. The separate maintenance agreement shall specify the fee, scope of support, support hours, response targets, exclusions, hosting responsibilities, billing arrangements, duration and termination rights.

8.5.4. If no maintenance agreement has been signed by the end of the Hypercare Period, the Service Provider's included hypercare obligations shall expire at the end of that period. Neither Party shall be obliged to enter into a maintenance arrangement, and no ongoing maintenance fee shall arise solely from the expiry of hypercare.
