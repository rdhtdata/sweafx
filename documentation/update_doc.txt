# 1. DOCUMENT PURPOSE

This document consolidates the current operational process followed by SweafEx, based on:

- Department-level process observations
- Existing Excel sheets
- Vendor and broker CRM formats
- Requirement and enquiry sheets
- Vehicle placement sheets
- Trip confirmation sheets
- Trip status sheets
- Supplier/payment records
- Rate cards
- Purchasing memos
- Existing WhatsApp communication formats
- Operational notes collected from SweafEx employees

The purpose of this document is to establish a clear understanding of:

1. How a transportation requirement enters SweafEx.
2. How the requirement is processed.
3. How vendors, brokers, vehicles and drivers are sourced.
4. How vehicle placement takes place.
5. How trips are monitored.
6. How information moves between departments.
7. How WhatsApp is currently used.
8. What Excel data is currently maintained.
9. How POD, billing and supplier payments are handled.
10. How vendor performance is tracked.
11. What information the future software must centralize.

The existing process is currently distributed across **WhatsApp, Excel sheets, vendor databases, requirement sheets, placement sheets, trip confirmation sheets, trip status sheets, supplier/payment sheets, CRM sheets, rate cards and purchasing memos**.

The objective of the proposed software is therefore not simply to digitize individual Excel sheets.

It is to create a **single operational system that connects the entire transportation journey**.

# 2. BUSINESS OVERVIEW

SweafEx operates as a transportation coordination and freight management company.

A customer approaches SweafEx when transportation is required for moving goods from one location to another.

The customer provides the transportation requirement, which can include:

- Pickup location
- Drop location
- Cargo/material
- Cargo weight
- Required vehicle size
- Vehicle type
- Loading date/time
- Budget or target price
- Other transportation requirements

SweafEx then identifies an appropriate transportation provider.

The provider may be:

- A fleet company
- A transportation vendor
- A broker
- An individual truck owner
- An individual driver

SweafEx coordinates the entire journey between the customer and the transportation provider.

The company's responsibility therefore extends beyond simply finding a vehicle.

It includes:

**Customer Requirement → Vendor Search → Quote → Vehicle Selection → Verification → Placement → Trip Confirmation → Loading → Transit → Unloading → POD → Payment → Billing → Performance/Ratings**

The supplied operational documentation confirms the broader workflow as:

**Client Requirement → Enquiry → Vendor Search → Vendor Quotes → Vendor Selection → Vehicle/Driver Verification → Placement → Trip Confirmation → Trip Tracking → Delivery/POD → Supplier Payment → Trip Completion → Rating/CRM**.

---

# 3. CORE BUSINESS PRINCIPLE

The future system should be designed around the idea that the company is managing **one continuous transportation journey**.

The journey begins with a customer requirement and ends only after:

- Delivery has occurred.
- POD has been received.
- Supplier-side financial processing has been completed.
- Customer billing/payment has been handled.
- Trip/vendor performance has been recorded.

The system should therefore follow:

```text
CUSTOMER
   ↓
ENQUIRY
   ↓
REQUIREMENT
   ↓
VENDOR SEARCH
   ↓
QUOTES
   ↓
VENDOR SELECTION
   ↓
VEHICLE + DRIVER
   ↓
VERIFICATION
   ↓
PLACEMENT
   ↓
TRIP
   ↓
TRIP MONITORING
   ↓
LOADING
   ↓
TRANSIT
   ↓
UNLOADING
   ↓
POD
   ↓
TRIP COMPLETION
   ↓
SUPPLIER PAYMENT
   ↓
CUSTOMER BILLING / PAYMENT
   ↓
RATING / CRM
```

This lifecycle is supported by the company's existing documentation.

# 5. DEPARTMENT RESPONSIBILITIES

## 5.1 SALES TEAM

### Primary responsibility

Sales is the primary customer-facing department.

The Sales Team handles incoming enquiries and maintains communication with customers.

### Responsibilities

Sales:

- Receives customer transportation enquiries.
- Communicates with customers.
- Collects customer requirements.
- Understands the transportation requirement.
- Passes the requirement into the vendor-search/procurement workflow.
- Receives vehicle information from Purchase/Vehicle Placement.
- Communicates vehicle information to the customer.
- Updates customers on trip progress.
- Communicates relevant operational updates received from Traffic.

### Information Sales works with

Typical requirement information includes:

- Customer/party
- Pickup location
- Destination
- Vehicle size
- Vehicle type
- Cargo/material
- Weight
- Loading date/time
- Target price/budget

The existing enquiry documentation confirms these as key fields.

---

# 6. VEHICLE PLACEMENT TEAM

## Primary responsibility

The Vehicle Placement Team is responsible for finding an appropriate vehicle/vendor to fulfil the customer's requirement.

They communicate with:

- Vendors
- Brokers
- Fleet companies
- Individual vehicle owners/drivers

### Responsibilities

The team:

1. Receives the transportation requirement.
2. Searches for available vehicles.
3. Contacts potential vendors.
4. Negotiates/collects vendor rates.
5. Determines vendor availability.
6. Evaluates potential transportation solutions.
7. Selects a suitable vehicle/vendor.
8. Collects required vendor and vehicle documents.
9. Passes vehicle/vendor information to the relevant operational team.
10. Informs Sales once the vehicle is placed.

### Responsibilities

- Payment-related activities

- Supply sheet

- Passing enquiry information to the vendor-handling team
- Coordinating vehicle arrangements
- Receiving vehicle information from Vehicle Placement
- Communicating vehicle availability to Sales

# 8. SALES BACKEND

Sales Backend takes over after a vehicle has been placed and supports operational communication and documentation.

### Responsibilities

Sales Backend:

- Sends vehicle information through WhatsApp.
- Sends trip-status information.
- Provides loading information.
- Provides unloading information.
- Supports operational communication.
- Creates/processes the final bill according to the current workflow.

The internal documentation specifically identifies WhatsApp communication around vehicle placement and trip status.

---

# 9. TRAFFIC COORDINATOR

## Primary responsibility

The Traffic Coordinator manages the trip after vehicle placement and operational handover.

The Traffic Coordinator effectively becomes the operational owner of the journey.

### Responsibilities

The Traffic Coordinator:

- Takes operational control of the trip.
- Coordinates with the driver.
- Coordinates with the vendor.
- Monitors vehicle movement.
- Coordinates loading.
- Coordinates unloading.
- Receives driver updates.
- Communicates relevant updates to Sales.
- Enables Sales to update the customer.
- Tracks trip milestones.
- Handles operational exceptions.

The existing Trip Status documentation confirms that the trip is tracked through stages such as Start, Loading, Transit, Reached, Unloading, POD Received and Trip Completed.

---

# 10. PURCHASE MANAGER

After completion of the transportation journey, the Purchase Manager becomes involved in the supplier/trip completion process.

### Responsibilities

The Purchase Manager:

- Reviews completed trips.
- Reviews driver/vendor performance.
- Handles trip/vendor rating.
- Participates in final billing.
- Processes supplier-side financial information.

The existing documentation contains a Rating field and vendor-performance information including response time and reliability rating.

---

# 11. ACCOUNTING / FINANCE

The Accounting Team manages the financial side of the completed transportation process.

### Responsibilities

The Accounting Team:

- Tracks customer payments.
- Tracks outstanding bills.
- Approves bills/payments in Zoho.
- Handles payment-related processing.
- Maintains accounting records.

### Zoho

Zoho remains an important part of the financial process.

Therefore, the proposed operational CRM should **work alongside Zoho**, rather than assuming that Zoho will be replaced.

The CRM should primarily manage operational information while financial/accounting information continues to flow through the existing financial system.

# STAGE 3 — REQUIREMENT

- Enquiry Number
- Date
- Customer/Party
- Origin
- Destination
- Vehicle size
- Vehicle type
- Material
- Weight
- Loading date
- Trip rate
- Status

The distinction between:

**Enquiry → Requirement → Trip**

is important.

An enquiry is the incoming request.

A requirement is the structured operational task being worked on.

A trip is the confirmed transportation assignment.

# STAGE 4 — RATE / VENDOR SEARCH

The Vehicle Placement/Purchase function searches for available vendors.

- Vehicles
- Availability
- Service levels

The existing format contains vendor quote information including:

```text
Vendor
Rate
Availability
```

The future software should not artificially restrict the number of quotes.

---

# STAGE 5 — RATE CARD COMPARISON

SweafEx maintains rate cards.

An example is a:

**Bangalore → Chennai Rate Card**

with fields such as:

- Vehicle Size
- Vehicle Type
- Weight
- Target Rate
- Rate
- Maximum Rate



The system should allow the employee to compare:

```text
Customer Target / Expected Rate
             +
SweafEx Rate Card
             +
Vendor Quotes
             ↓
      Commercial Decision
```

The lowest vendor rate should **not automatically be assumed to be the winning rate** unless SweafEx confirms this as a business rule.

Other considerations may include:

- Availability
- Reliability
- Response time
- Vehicle verification
- Driver verification
- Previous performance

These are present in the current data, but the exact vendor-selection formula remains to be confirmed.

# 35. CRITICAL OBSERVATIONS FOR CRM DESIGN

## Observation 1 — The Trip is the central operational object

Although the process begins with an enquiry, the **Trip / EXP ID** becomes the common reference connecting almost every department.

Therefore, the CRM should make the Trip the central operational record.

---

## Observation 2 — Departments should work on the same record

Sales should not maintain one version of a trip.

Purchase should not maintain another.

Traffic should not maintain another.

Accounts should not maintain another.

All departments should access the same underlying Trip record, with permissions controlling what each department can edit.

---

## Observation 3 — WhatsApp is currently part of the workflow

WhatsApp is not merely informal communication.

It is currently being used to:

- Receive requirements.
- Communicate vehicle information.
- Communicate trip status.
- Communicate loading/unloading information.
- Coordinate with vendors/drivers.
- Update customers.

Therefore, WhatsApp needs to be treated as a proper communication component of the future system.

---

## Observation 4 — Excel is currently being used as a database

Many existing Excel sheets are effectively functioning as databases.

The software should move the underlying data into a proper relational database.

Excel should become an:

**Import / Export / Reporting format**

rather than the primary operational database.

---

## Observation 5 — Vendor intelligence is valuable

SweafEx is already collecting:

- Response time
- Number of trips
- Reliability
- Vehicle information
- Driver information
- Ratings

This means the future CRM can eventually become more intelligent about vendor selection.

For example:

```text
Requirement
     ↓
Available Vendors
     ↓
Price
+
Availability
+
Response Time
+
Reliability
+
Past Performance
     ↓
Recommended Vendors
```

The actual recommendation algorithm should be introduced only after the business rules are clearly defined.

---

## Observation 6 — Verification should happen before placement

The current workflow already contains vehicle and driver verification fields.

Therefore:

```text
Vendor Selected
      ↓
Vehicle/Driver Verification
      ↓
Verification Passed
      ↓
Placement
```

should be a controlled workflow.

---

## Observation 7 — Trip monitoring should be event-based

Instead of one Excel row with many status columns, the system should maintain a chronological Trip Timeline.

This makes it possible to answer:

- When did the trip start?
- When did the truck reach loading?
- How long was loading?
- When did transit begin?
- When did the vehicle reach destination?
- When did unloading finish?
- When was POD received?
- Who updated each event?

```text
                SWEAFEX OPERATIONS CRM
                         │

       │                 │                 │
   OPERATIONS        COMMUNICATION       CRM
       │                 │                 │
       │              WhatsApp             │
       │                                   │

                         │
                  CENTRAL DATABASE
                         │
          ┌──────────────┼──────────────┐
          │              │              │
       REPORTS       DOCUMENTS        ZOHO
```

The purpose of the platform is therefore not merely to replace Excel.

It is to create a **single operational control system for SweafEx's entire transportation journey**.

Every enquiry, vendor, vehicle, driver, placement, Trip/EXP, status update, POD, payment, communication and performance record should be connected.

The central principle should be:

# ONE REQUIREMENT → ONE TRIP → ONE EXP ID → ONE COMPLETE OPERATIONAL HISTORY

This becomes the foundation for the detailed software requirements, database design, department dashboards, automation rules, WhatsApp integration and API integrations.
