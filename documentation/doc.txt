1 Executive Summary
SweafEx (Whooosh Networks Pvt Ltd) is a tech-enabled freight brokerage operating across South India, arranging
Full Truck Load (FTL) movements using outsourced vendor fleets. The company currently manages its entire
operation — enquiry to payment — across 8 WhatsApp groups, multiple Excel sheets, and Zoho Books (for PO only).
This BRD defines requirements for a unified CRM platform that digitises and owns the complete trip lifecycle. Every
requirement in this document is derived directly from the live 15-step SOP documented in March 2026.
Business Objectives
Objective What It Solves
Replace 8 WhatsApp groups Structured workflows replace group chats as operational systems. Groups remain for
human conversation only.
One Trip ID, everywhere System-generated EXP number auto-populates all records. Manual Trip ID entry
eliminated — the single biggest source of current errors.
Single source of truth Zoho is the record. WhatsApp is communication. Any data that exists only in WhatsApp
does not officially exist.
Customer self-service Customers view trip status, download TCN, and receive updates without calling — via
portal or WhatsApp API.
Vendor digital onboarding Vendors submit documents, confirm trips, update milestones, and upload POD via a
simple portal link — no app install.
Payment compliance All vendor payments via registered bank accounts. TDS deduction automated. UPI to
personal accounts eliminated.
Margin visibility Every trip shows: customer rate, vendor cost, gross margin. Currently invisible to
management in real time.
Scope
In Scope — Phase 1:
• Enquiry capture from all channels (WhatsApp, Website, Instagram, Facebook, Phone)
• Internal quote management and rate board
• Order creation and vehicle arrangement workflow
• Vendor master with document management
• Trip management — full milestone tracking
• Purchase Order generation and approval
• Trip Confirmation Note (TCN) auto-generation
• POD collection, tracking and submission
• Vendor payment — advance and balance with TDS
• Customer invoicing and payment collection
• WhatsApp Business API integration for all customer-facing messages
• Customer portal — trip status and document access
• Reporting dashboards

Key Rule — WhatsApp Business Access
Sales Backend shares the Company WhatsApp Business number with Sales Coordinator. Sales Backend sends all
document-based and routine messages (TCN, invoice, POD, payment reminders) directly to customers. Sales
Coordinator handles all inbound calls and outbound calls. Sales Backend's personal phone never contacts
customers. This keeps the Sales Coordinator free for negotiation and relationship work while routine communication
runs without interruption.

4 System Modules
13 modules. Each maps to a specific step in the SOP. Sequence reflects the order of first use in the trip lifecycle.
M01
Enquiry Management
Captures all inbound freight enquiries from every channel. Auto-generates ENQ number. Routes to Purchase
Coordinator. Stores closure reason on non-conversion.
Owner: Sales Coordinator + Sales Backend
M02
Customer Master
Stores all customer profiles: company name, billing address, GSTIN, contact persons, payment terms, POD
requirement (Yes/No), invoicing preference. Linked to all enquiries, orders and invoices.
Owner: Sales Backend
M03
Vendor Master
Stores all vendor/carrier profiles: name, type (broker/agent/owner/owner-driver), vehicle details, PAN, GSTIN, bank
account, TDS status, document repository with expiry alerts, trip history, ratings. Documents: RC, DL, PAN,
Passbook, Photo.
Owner: Purchase Coordinator
M04
Quote & Rate Board
Purchase Coordinator logs vendor quotes against each enquiry (up to 4 vendors). Operations Manager sets
customer rate range on the Rate Board. Sales Coordinator uses Rate Board guidance to negotiate. All quote history
stored against ENQ record.
Owner: Purchase Coordinator + Operations Manager
M05
Order Management
Converts confirmed enquiries to Orders (ORD number). Records confirmed customer rate. Links to vehicle
arrangement. Status pipeline: Pending > Arranged > In Transit > Delivered > Invoiced > Paid > Closed.
Owner: Sales Coordinator + Purchase Coordinator
M06
Trip Management
Core operational module. Auto-generates EXP Trip ID on vehicle placement. Stores all trip data: vehicle, driver,
route, dates, status milestones with timestamps. Links vendor, customer, order, PO, TCN, POD, and payments in
one record.
Owner: Purchase Coordinator
Confidential — For Engineering & Implementation Teams Only Page 6

M07
Document Management
Stores and tracks all trip and vendor documents. Vendor docs: RC, DL, PAN, Passbook, vehicle photo. Trip docs:
pre/post loading photos, signed TCN (POD), material bill, e-way bill. Alerts on document expiry (RC, DL validity).
Owner: Purchase Coordinator + Sales Backend
M08
Trip Confirmation Note (TCN)
Auto-generates TCN PDF from trip data when Loading Started milestone is triggered. Pre-fills all sections including
goods description from enquiry. Sales Backend sends to customer via Company WhatsApp Business. Signed TCN
returned by driver becomes the POD.
Owner: Sales Backend
M09
Purchase Order & Payments
Generates URPM purchasing memo in Zoho against Trip ID. Approval workflow: Purchase Coordinator creates >
Accounts approves in system > Finance pays to registered bank account. Records advance (90%) and balance
(10%) separately. TDS deduction automated per vendor setting. Payment receipts auto-generated.
Owner: Purchase Coordinator + Accounts
M10
POD Management
Tracks POD through 5 statuses: Not Collected > Soft Copy Received > Hard Copy In Transit > Hard Copy at Office
> Submitted to Customer. POD-NA flag set at order stage. Balance payment triggered by POD status change.
Overdue POD dashboard for management.
Owner: Purchase Coordinator + Sales Backend
M11
Customer Portal
External web interface for customers (no app required). Features: real-time trip status, trip milestone timeline, TCN
download, invoice view and download, POD acknowledgement. Accessible via WhatsApp link — no login friction for
field customers.
Owner: Sales Backend
M12
Vendor Portal
External web interface for vendors and drivers (no app required). Features: document upload for onboarding, trip
confirmation, milestone updates (Started / Loading / Unloading / Completed), POD photo upload. Accessible via
WhatsApp link.
Owner: Purchase Coordinator

M13
Billing, Invoicing & Reporting
Daily trip billing sheet auto-built from completed trips. Operations Manager approves. Sales Backend generates
GST invoice in Zoho — Accounts approves — Zoho auto-emails customer. Payment tracking with automated Day 7

5 Detailed Functional Requirements
5.1 Enquiry Management (M01)
Req ID Requirement ENQ-01 System captures enquiries from: WhatsApp Business API, Website form, Instagram DM API,
Facebook Messenger API, Manual entry by Sales Coordinator
ENQ-02 Auto-generate ENQ number (format: ENQ001, ENQ002…) on creation — never editable ENQ-03 Mandatory fields: Loading City+Pincode, Unloading City+Pincode, Vehicle Size, Vehicle Type,
Weight, Material, Loading Date, Party Name, Source Channel
ENQ-04 Status pipeline: New > Sent to Purchase > Quotes Received > Rate Set > Confirmed (Order
Created) > Closed
ENQ-05 Closure reason mandatory dropdown when status = Closed: Rate Difference / Customer
Arranged Own Vehicle / Vehicle Not Available / Customer Unresponsive / Other
ENQ-06 Edit log: every field change recorded with who changed it, what changed, and timestamp ENQ-07 Auto-acknowledge: WhatsApp reply sent to customer within 60 seconds of enquiry receipt
from WhatsApp channel
ENQ-08 Party name visible internally but not included in vendor-facing messages generated by system 5.2 Quote & Rate Board (M04)
Req ID Requirement QRB-01 Purchase Coordinator logs up to 4 vendor quotes per enquiry: Vendor Name, Quoted Rate,
Availability, Vehicle Number (optional), Quote Expiry
QRB-02 System identifies best rate automatically from logged quotes QRB-03 Operations Manager sets Customer Rate Min and Max on Rate Board — visible to Sales
Coordinator only, not Purchase team or vendors
QRB-04 Rate Board shows all active enquiries with: route, vehicle spec, vendor best rate, customer
rate range, margin %
QRB-05 Confirmed customer rate logged at order creation — system calculates and stores gross
margin per trip
QRB-06 Alert if Sales attempts to confirm order at rate below Rate Board minimum — requires
Operations Manager override
5.3 Vehicle Arrangement & Vendor Verification (M03 + M06)
Req ID Requirement VEH-01 Vendor Master stores: Name, Type, PAN, GSTIN, Bank Account (Name + IFSC + Account
No), TDS status, all documents with upload date and expiry date
