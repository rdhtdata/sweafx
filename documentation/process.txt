# SweafEx
## Current-State Business Process & Department Workflow

### 1. Business Overview

SweafEx provides transportation and freight coordination services to customers who require vehicles to transport goods from one location to another.

The basic business model is:

**Customer Requirement → Vehicle/Vendor Sourcing → Vehicle Placement → Trip Execution → Trip Monitoring → POD → Billing & Payment**

SweafEx generally does not operate the vehicles itself. Instead, when a customer approaches SweafEx with a transportation requirement, the company identifies and coordinates with suitable external transportation providers.

These providers may include:

- Fleet companies
- Transport brokers
- Individual truck owners/drivers
- Other transportation vendors

SweafEx's role is therefore to act as the coordination and management layer between the **customer** and the **transportation vendor/driver**.

The company remains involved throughout the journey, from the initial customer enquiry through vehicle arrangement, loading, transportation, unloading, documentation, billing and payment.

# 3. Departments & Responsibilities

## 3.1 Sales Team

### Primary Responsibility

The Sales Team is the primary customer-facing function.

They handle incoming transportation enquiries and maintain communication with customers throughout the process.

### Activities

The Sales Team:

- Receives incoming customer enquiries.
- Communicates with customers regarding their transportation requirements.
- Collects information required to fulfil the transportation request.
- Communicates with customers during the vehicle-placement process.
- Receives information from the Purchase / Vehicle Placement function regarding available vehicles.
- Updates customers about vehicle availability and progress.
- Continues customer communication during the trip.
- Provides customers with relevant updates received from the operational teams.

### Information handled

A customer requirement may include information such as:

- Cargo requirements
- Cargo weight
- Pickup location
- Drop location
- Budget
- Other transportation requirements

The exact enquiry information to be captured should be confirmed during the detailed requirements phase.

---

# 3.2 Vehicle Placement Team

### Primary Responsibility

The Vehicle Placement Team is responsible for finding a suitable transportation solution for a customer requirement.

They communicate with external transportation providers, including:

- Vendors
- Brokers
- Fleet companies
- Individual truck drivers

### Activities

The Vehicle Placement Team:

1. Receives the transportation requirement.
2. Searches for suitable vendors/vehicles.
3. Communicates with vendors to determine availability.
4. Evaluates possible transportation solutions.
5. Selects a suitable vehicle/vendor solution.
6. Collects required vendor and vehicle documentation.
7. Sends the collected information to the Traffic Coordinator / relevant operational team.
8. Informs the Sales Team once a vehicle has been successfully arranged.

### Vendor documentation

Before vehicle placement, the required vendor information/documents may include:

- RC
- Driver's Licence (DL)
- PAN
- Aadhaar
- TDS Certificate
- GST Certificate
- Bank Details
- Company-related documentation, where applicable

The exact mandatory document checklist should be confirmed with the business.

### Key principle

**A vehicle should not be considered ready for placement until the required vendor/vehicle information has been collected.**

---

# 3.3 Purchase Coordinator

### Primary Responsibility

The Purchase Coordinator supports the procurement and vendor-arrangement workflow.

### Activities

The Purchase Coordinator is involved in:

- Payment-related activities
- Procurement workflow
- Supply sheet management
- Vendor sheet management
- Receiving/processing enquiry information
- Passing enquiry requirements to the team responsible for finding vendors
- Coordinating vehicle arrangements with the Vehicle Placement Team
- Communicating vehicle availability to the Sales Team

### Relationship with Vehicle Placement

The Purchase Coordinator acts as an important coordination point between:

**Customer Requirement → Vendor Search → Vehicle Arrangement → Sales**

The exact division of responsibility between the Purchase Coordinator and Vehicle Placement Team should be documented further, since the current notes indicate that the two functions work closely together.

---

# 3.4 Sales Backend

### Primary Responsibility

Sales Backend takes over after a vehicle has been placed and supports the operational communication and documentation process.

### Activities

Once a vehicle has been placed, Sales Backend:

- Sends vehicle-related information through WhatsApp.
- Sends trip-status information through WhatsApp.
- Provides loading information.
- Provides unloading information.
- Supports communication of operational updates.
- Creates the final bill.

### Communication

Sales Backend appears to act as an important communication bridge between the operational teams and external parties.

The current process uses WhatsApp for communication related to:

- Vehicle outsourcing
- Trip status
- Loading
- Unloading
- Other operational updates

The exact WhatsApp groups, recipients and message types should be documented separately.

---

# 3.5 Traffic Coordinator

### Primary Responsibility

The Traffic Coordinator manages the transportation journey after the vehicle has been arranged and the operational process has been handed over from Sales Backend.

The Traffic Coordinator is responsible for monitoring and coordinating the trip from the beginning of the journey through loading, transportation and unloading.

### Activities

The Traffic Coordinator:

- Takes operational control of the trip.
- Coordinates with the driver/vendor.
- Monitors the vehicle's journey.
- Coordinates the loading process.
- Coordinates the unloading process.
- Receives trip-related information from the driver/vendor.
- Passes relevant information to Sales.
- Enables Sales to provide customer updates.
- Tracks the operational progress of the vehicle.

### Loading and vendor creation

Once the vehicle is loaded:

1. The vendor needs to be created/registered in the system.
2. A Purchase Order needs to be created.
3. A memo is associated with the Purchase Order.
4. The Purchase Order goes through an approval process.
5. Vendor payment is then processed according to the applicable workflow.

### Trip completion

Once the trip is completed:

- The Traffic Coordinator obtains the POD.
- The POD is provided by the driver.
- The POD is used as evidence of completed transportation.
- The completed trip is then passed back into the purchase/financial workflow.

---

# 3.6 Driver / Vendor

The driver or vendor is the external transportation provider responsible for physically executing the transportation requirement.

### Responsibilities

The driver/vendor:

- Accepts the transportation requirement.
- Provides vehicle and driver information.
- Provides required documentation.
- Reports vehicle/trip progress.
- Coordinates with the Traffic Coordinator.
- Provides loading/unloading information.
- Executes the transportation journey.
- Provides the POD after completion.

### POD

The Proof of Delivery (POD) is provided by the driver after the delivery is completed.

The POD consists of a signed document/page confirming delivery.

The POD is an important completion document for the trip and is subsequently used in the billing/financial process.

---

# 3.7 Purchase Manager

### Primary Responsibility

The Purchase Manager becomes involved after completion of the transportation journey and is responsible for the vendor/trip evaluation and final billing process.

### Activities

After the trip is completed, the Purchase Manager:

- Reviews the completed trip.
- Provides/runs a rating for the trip, driver and/or vendor.
- Uses the applicable rating methodology/formula.
- Processes the final bill.

### Trip / Driver / Vendor Rating

The current notes indicate that the rating may be based on factors such as:

**Response Time**

Potentially:

- Driver response time
- Vendor response time

The exact rating formula is not yet defined in the notes and should be confirmed with SweafEx.

---

# 3.8 Accounting Team

### Primary Responsibility

The Accounting Team manages the financial follow-up after billing.

### Activities

The Accounting Team:

- Tracks payment of the final customer bill.
- Handles payment-related follow-up.
- Approves payments and bills.
- Uses Zoho for financial processing.
- Maintains the financial records in Zoho.

### Zoho

Zoho is currently used as part of the financial workflow, particularly for:

- Bills
- Payments
- Approvals
- Accounting-related activities

The operational CRM should therefore work alongside Zoho rather than necessarily replacing it.

# 4. End-to-End Process

## Stage 1 – Customer Enquiry

The process begins when a customer contacts SweafEx with a transportation requirement.

The customer provides the required transportation information, which may include:

- Pickup location
- Drop location
- Cargo
- Cargo weight
- Budget
- Other transportation requirements

The Sales Team receives and manages the enquiry.

### Output

**Transportation Requirement / Enquiry**

---

# Stage 2 – Vendor & Vehicle Search

The requirement is passed to the procurement/vehicle-placement function.

The Vehicle Placement Team searches the available vendor network for a suitable transportation solution.

They communicate with:

- Fleet operators
- Brokers
- Individual drivers
- Other vendors

The objective is to find a vehicle that can fulfil the customer's requirement at a suitable commercial arrangement.

### Output

**Potential Vehicle + Vendor**

---

# Stage 3 – Vendor & Vehicle Documentation

Before vehicle placement, the required vendor and vehicle documents are collected.

Typical documents include:

- RC
- DL
- PAN
- Aadhaar
- TDS Certificate
- GST Certificate
- Bank Details

The collected information is passed to the relevant operational team.

### Output

**Documented & Ready Vendor/Vehicle**

---

# Stage 4 – Vehicle Placement

Once a suitable vehicle has been identified and the necessary information has been collected, the vehicle is placed against the customer requirement.

The Vehicle Placement/Purchase function informs Sales about the selected vehicle.

Sales then communicates the vehicle details to the customer.

### Output

**Vehicle Placed**

---

# Stage 5 – Operational Handover

After vehicle placement, the process moves into operational execution.

Sales Backend communicates relevant vehicle and trip information through WhatsApp and records/provides loading and unloading information.

The Traffic Coordinator then takes responsibility for monitoring the trip.

### Output

**Trip Under Operational Management**

---

# Stage 6 – Trip Execution

The Traffic Coordinator coordinates with the driver/vendor throughout the journey.

The trip includes:

**Vehicle Start → Pickup → Loading → Transit → Destination → Unloading**

The Traffic Coordinator collects operational updates and passes relevant information to Sales.

Sales uses these updates to keep the customer informed.

### Output

**Trip Progress + Customer Updates**

---

# Stage 7 – Purchase Order & Payment

After the vehicle has been loaded:

1. Vendor is created/registered.
2. Purchase Order is created.
3. Memo is attached/created.
4. Purchase Order goes through approval.
5. Vendor payment is processed according to the applicable workflow.

The exact sequence and approval authority should be confirmed.

### Output

**Approved Vendor PO + Payment Process**

---

# Stage 8 – Delivery & POD

The vehicle reaches the destination and completes unloading.

The driver provides the signed POD.

The POD is collected and recorded as part of the trip completion process.

### Output

**Completed Trip + POD**

---

# Stage 9 – Trip / Vendor Evaluation

Once the trip is completed, the Purchase Manager reviews the trip and vendor/driver performance.

The current process appears to include a rating mechanism.

One identified factor is:

**Response Time**

Potential rating subjects include:

- Driver
- Vendor
- Trip

The exact formula and scoring system require confirmation.

### Output

**Trip/Vendor/Driver Rating**

---

# Stage 10 – Billing

Following trip completion, the final bill is prepared.

The current notes indicate that Sales Backend / Purchase Manager are involved in creating the final bill.

The exact ownership of final bill creation should be confirmed.

### Output

**Final Customer Bill**

---

# Stage 11 – Accounts & Payment Tracking

The Accounting Team takes responsibility for financial follow-up.

They:

- Track customer payments.
- Approve bills/payments in Zoho.
- Maintain the financial records.
- Follow up on outstanding payments where required.

### Output

**Payment Recorded / Outstanding Payment Tracked**

# 6. Core Business Objects

Based on the current process, the future system will need to maintain information around several core objects.

### Customer

Information about the customer requesting transportation.

### Enquiry

The initial transportation requirement submitted by the customer.

### Vendor

The external transportation provider.

### Vehicle

The vehicle being used to fulfil the requirement.

### Driver

The individual operating the vehicle.

### Trip

The actual transportation journey.

### Documents

Documents associated with customers, vendors, vehicles, drivers and trips.

Examples include:

- RC
- DL
- PAN
- Aadhaar
- GST Certificate
- TDS Certificate
- Bank Details
- POD

### Purchase Order

The vendor-side commercial document created after the vehicle is loaded.

### POD

Proof of Delivery provided by the driver after delivery.

### Final Bill

The customer-side billing document generated after completion of the transportation process.

### Payment

Financial transactions associated with vendor payments and customer collections.
