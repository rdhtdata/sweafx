Structuring the Proven Business Model
SweafEx operates a functioning freight brokerage business handling ~10 trips/day with a 10-person team. The objective of this project is not to disrupt proven vendor relationships or sales practices, but to eliminate the manual friction around daily execution.
THE CORE PHILOSOPHY
"The work does not disappear — the wasted work does."
Eliminates copying rates between sheets, re-typing vehicle numbers, chasing drivers on phone calls for basic milestones, and searching through WhatsApp for POD photos.
Key Operational Outcomes:
  • Single Source of Operational Truth: One central live master sheet replaces isolated desktop Excel files.
  • Zero Duplicate Data Entry: Information entered at enquiry travels seamlessly to placement, TCN, and billing.
  • Higher Operational Capacity: Creates additional operational capacity for the existing team by reducing repetitive administrative work as trip volume grows.
External Systems: Zoho Books continues independently as SweafEx's financial accounting system; the platform handles operational execution.

Octagram Commercial Boundaries • Scope Protection Page 10
Commercial Boundaries
Not Included in Standard Maintenance
Clear definition of what constitutes new development versus standard operational maintenance.
"Maintenance keeps the agreed Phase 1 platform working. It does not represent unlimited future development."
New functionality, major workflow changes, and new integrations will always be discussed, scoped, and approved separately before additional development begins.
1. NEW FEATURES & MODULES
Features and capabilities outside the agreed Phase 1 deliverable:
  • New standalone modules & departmental workspaces
  • New custom analytics dashboards & financial widgets
  • New custom operational or financial report exports
  • New automated workflows & multi-rule business logic
  • New multi-level approval hierarchies & audit chains
  • New user role profiles & granular permission sets
  • New external portal capabilities or standalone portals
Treated as standalone feature additions and quoted separately upon request.
2. NEW INTEGRATIONS
Third-party software systems outside Phase 1 scope:
  • Zoho Books direct two-way API synchronization
  • New external accounting, tally or ERP connectors
  • New external third-party freight software APIs
  • New WhatsApp workflows outside agreed Phase 1 scope
  • Conversational AI / interactive chatbot customer journeys
  • Online payment gateway & payout integrations
  • SMS, email marketing, or alternate notification gateways
Evaluated as Phase 2 enhancement projects based on operational demand.
3. MAJOR CHANGES & TECH
High-complexity structural architectural modifications:
  • Major UI/UX redesigns or structural screen restyling
  • Rebuilding existing core database schemas or models
  • Major alterations to agreed business workflows
  • Native Android & iOS App Store applications
  • Real-time IoT hardware GPS telematics tracking
  • AI algorithmic matching or machine learning models
  • Major cloud infrastructure migrations or rebuilds
Requires detailed technical re-architecture and standalone estimation.
Mutual Protection Principle: Requests that introduce new functionality or materially change the existing system will be reviewed, estimated and quoted separately before development begins. This protects SweafEx from unpredictable ongoing costs while preserving clear engineering standards.
